# India Today-inspired redesign research

## Adaptation boundary
Tradingle will adopt the observed editorial information architecture, density, responsive behavior, and component relationships from India Today. It will not copy India Today’s logo, proprietary brand assets, exact copy, article content, or third-party imagery.

## Observed patterns
1. A thin network bar at the top establishes the publishing group and current date.
2. The masthead uses a strong red wordmark, edition selector, magazine/live-TV/search/sign-in utilities, and a persistent left navigation rail on desktop.
3. A topic-trend strip sits below the masthead and uses pill-shaped links for current topics.
4. The main content is a narrow editorial canvas beside a persistent pale-grey ad rail. The lead story is a white card with a bold sans-serif headline, short deck, large image, and compact metadata/actions.
5. Homepage modules repeat a clear card grammar: red section eyebrow, 2–3 line headline, 1–2 line summary, optional thumbnail, and light-grey borders/shadows. Modules are stacked vertically rather than presented as one oversized hero.
6. The homepage mixes news-analysis cards, latest-news cards, topic carousels, video/fact-check modules, and language/network modules. The right rail is reserved for ads, newsletter promos, and trending video.
7. The footer is light and compact. It includes the wordmark, social links, trending/latest topic strips, a short utility navigation row, and copyright.
8. Content type hierarchy is driven by modest weight and spacing: lead headlines are strong but not giant, card headlines are readable at roughly 16–24px, metadata is small and muted, and red is used for section labels and active accents.

## Final size-audit actions
- Removed the extra Spotlight intro area from Tradingle’s homepage so the first editorial card starts immediately below the sticky category strip.
- Removed the duplicate Newsletters link from the network utility strip; the primary masthead action remains.
- Widened Tradingle’s desktop rail to 214px, added iconography, and added expandable groups for Business, Sports, TV & Magazine, and Other News.
- Added a sticky, thin category strip below the masthead.
- Changed the right rail to a square ad placeholder plus compact recommended cards with 72px square thumbnails.
- Explicitly placed the lead headline/deck before its moderate landscape image and added a visible copy block to prevent the image from dominating the first viewport.
