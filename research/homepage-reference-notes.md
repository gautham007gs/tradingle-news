# Homepage reference notes — 2026-10-13

## Indian Express
Source: https://indianexpress.com/

- The top navigation is a compact, single-line text bar with many section links rather than a tall branded hero.
- The primary story is a two-column editorial feature: headline and deck are readable but restrained, with a single large supporting image.
- Supporting headlines in the left rail are mostly text-led and separated by fine dotted/hairline rules; they do not all use oversized images.
- The page uses a clear editorial rhythm: lead story, podcasts/feature module, recap strip, section blocks, and latest news.
- Above-the-fold content is dense enough to expose several headlines without making every story a visual card.

## NDTV
Source: https://www.ndtv.com/

- The header separates edition selection, search, and destinations from the main news links.
- The navigation gives priority to Latest, India, Video, Podcast, Lifestyle, Profit, Movies, Cricket, Cities and other utility destinations.
- The information architecture is multi-level but the visual treatment is compact and utility-first.

## Design implication for Tradingle
- Keep one modest masthead row and one compact category nav row; avoid a tall dark hero/header.
- Reduce display headline scale to roughly 32–42px desktop for the lead and 18–26px for section cards; use 24–30px lead headline on mobile.
- Use images selectively: one lead image, small 16:9 section cards, and text-first rails. Avoid making every module a large image tile.
- Use a centered max-width around 1180–1240px, 20–28px gutters, 1px rules, and 16–24px section spacing.
- Keep all surfaces light; retain dark ink only for type/rules, with a restrained red accent for section labels and active states.

## Supporting design reference
Source: https://www.smashingmagazine.com/2008/11/newspaper-website-design-trends-and-examples/

- Newspaper homepages benefit from structured grids, tabbed/utility content areas, and categorized headline links that expose depth without competing visual weight.
- A single lead image plus text-led headline rails is a useful balance for news density and scanability.
